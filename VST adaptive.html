English Vocabulary Size Test

This task measures the size of your English vocabulary. For each question, choose the option closest in meaning to the capitalised word. Select I don't know if you do not know the word; please do not guess.

You will complete the same adaptive vocabulary test twice. Comparing the two results helps us examine the reliability of the test. Each test takes about 5 minutes.

Each item has a 45-second limit. After 15 seconds, you will be asked whether you know the word. If you choose to continue thinking, the timer starts again when you close that message.
